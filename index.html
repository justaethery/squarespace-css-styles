My Philosophy & Approach

When we work together, my absolute first priority is your comfort. This practice is a completely safe, welcoming, and hate-free space where everyone is honored and respected just as they are. I use the cards to ground the intuitive and visual information I receive, offering you a clear reflection of where you are right now. My intention is never to tell you what to do, but to provide the profound insight you need to confidently navigate your options, always honoring that you are the architect of your own life.
