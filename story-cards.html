The Story Behind the Cards

With roots stretching from Georgia to the Salem trials, interwoven with a beautiful mix of many cultures, my spiritual journey, and connection to this work, started long before my seven years of practicing tarot and the craft. Since I was little, I have always had a deep interest, and a natural tie to the spiritual realm. I naturally feel and connect with the energies around me, which translates into distinct visual and intuitive information that helps me understand a situation on a much deeper level.
